SOURCING

FROM SHADED LEAF TO JALAN SULTAN·JUL 30, 2026

Three weeks before harvest, the fields our matcha comes from disappear under shade cloth. Starved of direct sun, the leaves stretch, soften and load up on the compounds that make ceremonial grade taste sweet instead of sharp.

The journey

After steaming and drying, the leaf rests as tencha before it is ground slowly on granite stones, thirty grams an hour, into the powder we seal and fly in small batches. We buy little and often, so nothing sits in our store room longer than a season.

It lands in a shophouse that has sold tea in one form or another for most of a century. We like to think the building approves.
